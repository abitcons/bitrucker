Trading Risk Disclosure

Version {{ legal_version }}

You can lose some or all capital allocated to automated trading. Do not trade money you cannot afford to lose.

Automation and AI risk

Rules and AI can be wrong, stale, overfit, or unsuitable for current conditions. Human review remains necessary. A bot can continue executing consistently even when its assumptions are no longer valid.

Market and execution risk

  • Prices can gap, liquidity can disappear, and stop orders are not guaranteed execution prices.
  • Fees, spread, slippage, funding costs, taxes, and partial fills can turn an apparent gain into a net loss.
  • Exchange downtime, rate limits, API changes, credential revocation, and network failures can leave orders or positions unmanaged.
  • Low-liquidity assets can be manipulated and may become impossible to exit near the displayed price.

Crypto and tokenized-stock risk

Crypto assets may be highly volatile and can lose all value. Tokenized stocks are not necessarily direct shares: they may provide price exposure without voting, dividend, insolvency, or shareholder rights. They add issuer, custodian, smart-contract, depegging, jurisdiction, market-hours, and liquidity risks.

Backtest and paper-trading limits

Simulations may omit latency, order-book impact, delistings, changing fees, outages, taxes, and survivorship bias. Paper results are not live results.

Recommended controls

Begin in paper mode, use small allocations, set account and strategy loss limits, disable withdrawals, use IP allowlists, independently verify open orders, and retain direct access to the exchange emergency controls.
